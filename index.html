THE QUESTION

Adventure can mean a bushwalk close to home, a ride through a regional trail network, or a trip built around a cycling experience. Where is outdoor participation strongest, and how does it connect with Australia's tourism economy?

Two lenses, kept separate: AusPlay measures participation; Tourism Research Australia measures trips, nights and spending.

THE TAKEAWAY

One map can't tell the whole story.

Participation estimates show what people do; tourism statistics show the journeys and spending associated with travel. Read together, they reveal different dimensions of outdoor life in Australia — without pretending that participation automatically means tourism.

METHODS & SOURCES

What the data can — and can't — say.

The visual story uses public data and clearly separates participation, cycling-inclusive trips and general domestic tourism.

01

AusPlay · 2025

Adult activity participation rates and state-level estimates. Adult means age 15+. Participation is at least once in the previous 12 months. The 2024+ online method differs from the 2015–2023 phone method, so avoid direct long-run comparisons across that break.

Official AusPlay tables ↗
02

TRA · Cycle Tourism

Published October 2025, describing 2024 trips that included cycling. All nights and spending from a trip are counted if cycling occurred during it; the cycling activity's own causal contribution cannot be isolated.

Read the report ↗
03

TRA · Domestic tourism

State-level domestic trips, nights and expenditure. The National Visitor Survey was replaced from January 2025, with backcast figures used for comparisons. This source describes general domestic tourism, not adventure-specific tourism.

Tourism Research Australia ↗
04

Geography & reliability

State boundaries were supplied in GDA2020 and simplified to reduce page weight. Small activity estimates can be uncertain; the charts are descriptive and should not be used to claim meaningful differences without checking the relevant margin-of-error guidance.

Boundary dataset ↗
Limitations to keep in mind
  • AusPlay participation does not establish whether an activity occurred on a tourist trip.
  • TRA cycling-inclusive trips count the entire trip's spending and nights, not only cycling-related spending.
  • General tourism totals are context, not an estimate of adventure tourism.
  • Some cycling state values in the report are rounded. One South Australian nights value was not readable in the source figure and is deliberately omitted rather than guessed.
  • Relative margin-of-error guidance should be reviewed before making fine-grained claims about small state estimates.
